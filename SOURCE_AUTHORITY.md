# Source authority and translation scope

This release is a Spanish translation and edition of currently available, bounded SGA source material. It does not silently promote editorial conjectures into source authority.

- **SGA 1:** complete Spanish reader derived from the project’s sealed SGA 1 source and adjudication closure.
- **SGA 2:** Spanish reader through Exposé V. Its evidence preserves the corrected and diplomatic French branch surfaces, discrepancy ledger, correction referrals, terminology decisions, semantic dependencies, formula inventory, build receipts, visual review, and final R07 checker seal.
- **SGA 5:** complete published contents represented by the project’s immutable sealed 408-member release ZIP; its embedded checksum set validates 407/407 payload rows. A later loose-tree rights-wording overlay is not treated as the sealed baseline.
- **SGA 6:** a bounded translation tranche of Exposé X, printed pages 519–524, ending during a proof. It is not represented as a complete SGA 6 translation.

Rights-limited French scans, source trees, and third-party witnesses are not redistributed merely to make the evidence archive self-contained. The public provenance archive instead records reproducibility anchors, byte counts, hashes, bibliographic identifiers, and deterministic privacy transformations where available.

No separate DOI is assigned to SGA 1, SGA 2, SGA 5, SGA 6, or any exposé. These are components of the one evolving Spanish-SGA object.
